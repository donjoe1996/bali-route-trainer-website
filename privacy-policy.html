Bali Route Trainer — Privacy Policy

Effective 28 September 2026 · Applies to the Android app "Bali Route Trainer"

In short

The app does not collect, store on any server, sell or share personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. It does not use your device's location.

What the app sends over the internet

To draw the map and the terrain you drive on, the app downloads public map data from the services below. Like any internet request, each one reveals your device's IP address to that service, along with which part of the map you are looking at (the tile or area requested) and the app's name. The app sends nothing else — no identifiers, no account, no device data. These services are operated by third parties under their own privacy policies.

ServiceWhat for
OpenStreetMap tile servers (tile.openstreetmap.org)Map images on the area picker and minimap
Esri ArcGIS Online (server.arcgisonline.com)Backup map images if OpenStreetMap's are unavailable
Amazon Web Services, public terrain tiles (s3.amazonaws.com)Ground elevation (hills and valleys)
Public Overpass API servers (overpass-api.de, overpass.kumi.systems, maps.mail.ru, overpass.private.coffee)Road data, only when you pick an area outside the maps built into the app

Most of Bali's roads and buildings are built into the app, so ordinary play inside Bali needs only the map images and terrain above.

What is stored on your device

The app keeps downloaded map data and the last area you played in its own private storage on your device, so it loads faster next time. This never leaves your device. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

Permissions

The app asks only for internet access (to download the map data above). It does not request location, contacts, camera, microphone, storage or any other permission.

Donations

Some versions of the app show cryptocurrency addresses for voluntary donations. The app only displays these addresses and lets you copy them; it does not connect to any wallet, process payments or collect any information about donations. A donation unlocks nothing.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no personal data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new effective date.
