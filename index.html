Bali Route Trainer

Drive the real roads of Bali and learn your way around — from the rice terraces of Ubud to the streets of Denpasar and the rim of Batur.

The real island, road by road

Every road comes from OpenStreetMap and every hill from real elevation data. Pick a spot on the map and drive there.

What's in it

Free roam: no missions and no timers. Just drive, and the map sticks.

All of Bali built in

The whole island's roads and buildings ship inside the app. Road names show as you drive, so you learn them.

Real hills and valleys

Terrain from elevation data, with the roads draped over it: climb to Kintamani, wind down to the lakes.

Traffic and lights

Cars on the roads around you, traffic lights at junctions, and a WRONG WAY warning on one-way streets.

Day and night

The sun follows Bali's real clock. After dark, headlights light the road ahead.

Start anywhere

Begin in Ubud, Sanur or Denpasar, or drag the map and drop a pin wherever you want to drive.

Free, no ads, no tracking

No account, no analytics, no location access. See the privacy policy.

Install on Android

Until it's on Google Play, install the APK straight from GitHub.

  1. Download the APK on your phone with the button at the top of this page.
  2. Open it from your notifications or Downloads. If Android asks, allow your browser to install unknown apps.
  3. Tap Install, then open Bali Route Trainer and pick where to drive.

Says "App not installed" when updating? Test builds are signed with a new key each time, so uninstall the old copy first, then install the new one. The app needs an internet connection for map images and terrain; Bali's roads are already inside it.
